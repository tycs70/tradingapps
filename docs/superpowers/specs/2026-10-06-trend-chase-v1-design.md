## Intent
Retain sustained intraday trends in a separate watchlist after EMA9 crosses EMA21. This is a scanner/watchlist; there are no order or trade-exit actions. Preserve the normal provisional pre-cross scanner for future qualifying setups. Underlying NSE shares, five-minute candles, Upstox V3 feed, current option-eligible universe, symmetric LONG/SHORT logic.

## Trend lifecycle and ranking
At an actually observed live EMA9/EMA21 crossover, capture the signed EMA9 angle calculated using the existing fixed reference geometry. Qualify direction-aligned positive strength (LONG angle > 0; SHORT angle < 0), without another arbitrary minimum. Price must currently lie on the trend side of EMA9. Track every qualifying candidate, display five bullish and five bearish sorted by frozen crossover angle strength descending, then crossover time and symbol. Display current angle independently; it never changes the captured ranking score. No history/startup snapshot invents a live crossover.

Live entries are Provisional until the crossing five-minute candle has been verified as completed with the correct EMA relationship and its close on the trend side of EMA9. A live EMA crossover reversal removes the provisional candidate. Once confirmed, retain through wicks and intrabar price movement, until a completed five-minute candle closes strictly across EMA9. Equality is a touch, not a cross. If EMA9 crosses EMA21 in the opposite direction, remove the old candidate; the new direction must independently qualify. A removed candidate needs another actually observed EMA crossover to rejoin the trend watchlist; later pre-cross setups continue through the normal scanner.

Stale/missing quotes, disconnects and data gaps hide candidates from active Top 5 while preserving confirmed candidates internally. Discard uncertain provisional candidates when continuity is lost. Revalidate every completed candle since the last check, including broker backfill; any missed adverse close removes the candidate permanently even if price later recovers. Suppress historical reconstruction of crossover events. Same-day saved confirmed candidates can restore only after verified history and fresh quotes. If required revalidation bars are unavailable, do not display the candidate. Reset candidates each IST trading day; no yesterday membership leaks into today's list.
