## Independent review

A fresh whole-branch reviewer found five important issues: old quote replay after overflow recovery, prior-bucket ranking during history rollover, an obsolete EMA relation after a verified close, alert sounds lost behind unrelated events, and reuse of a previously authorized feed URL during reconnect. Six reproducing tests failed before fixes and passed afterward; the full 52-test suite passed. Confirmation timestamp accuracy was also fixed because a completion event previously carried the candle opening time. Verified closure now advances the observed relationship and records candle completion time. No review findings remain deferred.

The reviewer could not certify Windows COM/registry/installation behavior, authenticated broker semantics or actual laptop latency from Linux. The release therefore retains those explicit validation limits. The initial release's regression fixes were verified by their failing/passing tests and a full suite, without a second review pass.

The 1.0.1 update received a separate fresh read-only review covering Top 20 filtering, unchanged universe/strategy behavior, x64 DPAPI signatures and native memory release, encrypted-only persistence, masking, forgetting and failure paths. No Critical or Important issues were found. The reviewer independently ran the credential/UI checks; native DPAPI remained skipped in Linux.
